Your first comparison

  1. Choose a Left folder and a Right folder using Browse.
  2. Choose comparison criteria. Content hash (T2) compares fingerprints; Byte-exact (T3) compares file bytes.
  3. Select Compare and inspect the results.
  4. Build a synchronization plan. Check its direction and operations, resolve conflicts, then approve it when ready.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Maintain an independent backup of important files.

Common questions

Why can’t the app access my folder?

Reconnect the original drive, then choose the folder again using Browse. SandingBerkas needs access to both selected folders. It will not silently substitute a different drive.

When do scheduled profiles run?

Keep SandingBerkas open when schedules are enabled. This release runs scheduled profiles while the app is open.

Where are recovery copies kept?

Review the protection policy before running a plan. Recovery copies may be kept in your chosen destination folder. Keep them until you are satisfied that you no longer need them.

Help us reproduce the problem

Include your SandingBerkas version, macOS version, the steps you took, what you expected, and what happened. Try to reproduce the issue with disposable sample files. Do not upload private documents, credentials, or unredacted reports.
