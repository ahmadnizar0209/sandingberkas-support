Your files & privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

SandingBerkas compares and synchronizes folders you choose on your Mac. The Mac App Store edition does not collect personal data, include advertising or analytics, or transmit your files to the developer. No account is required.

Folder access

You choose folders through the macOS file picker. SandingBerkas uses security-scoped bookmarks to remember permission to those locations. It reads filenames, paths, sizes, dates, selected metadata and file contents as needed for comparison, previews, verification and the file operations you request. It checks available disk space before operations. Folder permission is not permission to execute a synchronization: review the proposed plan before approving it, or explicitly enable a scheduled profile.

Information stored on your Mac

Preferences, folder bookmarks, comparison state, content fingerprints, synchronization baselines and run history are stored locally in the application's storage. Saved comparison documents, profiles and exported reports may include folder paths, filenames and metadata. Recovery copies and versions may be stored within your chosen destination folders. These are not uploaded to the developer.

Locations and other applications you select

If you select a network share or a folder managed by a cloud storage provider, reading or writing that folder uses that service through macOS. Its handling of data is governed by that provider. Revealing a file, opening it in another application, using Finder Get Info, or saving and sharing a report may disclose information to the destination you choose.

Optional features

Notifications are requested through macOS. Scheduling runs while the app is open unless a separately configured background helper has been explicitly authorized. Local declarative plugin recipes can access the result or selection metadata described in Settings. They cannot load native libraries or execute arbitrary scripts. Store updates are provided by Apple through the Mac App Store.

Control and retention

You can stop a run, disable schedules and plugins, and manage local caches in Settings. Keep recovery copies until you are satisfied that you no longer need them. Removing the app does not automatically remove saved documents, reports or recovery copies in your folders. macOS controls permissions and the application container.

Changes

Any change in the app's collection or processing behavior requires an updated policy and App Store privacy disclosure.

Contact

For support and general privacy questions, open an issue in the public SandingBerkas support repository. GitHub account and issue information is handled by GitHub. Issues are public: do not include sensitive files, personal information, secrets or unredacted folder paths.
